# Reviewer Materials Checklist

This repository is intended to make the empirical workflow auditable. The manuscript is supported by the following materials.

## Code
- `run_models_only.py`: reruns the baseline, robustness, and article-results scripts.
- `scripts/05_baseline_models.py`: baseline panel model workflow.
- `scripts/06_robustness_models.py`: robustness checks and alternative specifications.
- `scripts/01_extract_pdf_texts.py`, `02_dictionary_content_analysis.py`, `03_refine_content_scores.py`, and `04_create_model_ready_dataset.py`: documented upstream workflow components.

## Data
- `data/raw/`: public quantitative input files used in the project, including SIPRI, WDI, NSIDC/OWID sea-ice data, and World Bank Pink Sheet prices.
- `data/processed/`: cleaned and analysis-ready datasets generated by the workflow.

## Outputs
- `outputs/Arctic_Panel_Baseline_Models_v1.xlsx`
- `outputs/Arctic_Robustness_Models_v1.xlsx`
- `outputs/Arctic_Article_Results_Package_v1.xlsx`

## Documentation
- `docs/Arctic_Content_Analysis_Codebook.docx`: scoring categories and validation logic.
- `docs/DOCUMENT_CORPUS_INDEX.md` and `docs/document_corpus_index.csv`: official document inventory and inclusion/exclusion status.
- `docs/Arctic_Results_Interpretation_Memo_v1.docx`: interpretation memo used to discipline the claims.

## Manuscript
- `manuscript/Arctic_Manuscript_Final_Human_Polished_Q1_v7.docx`: current polished manuscript draft.

## Strategic document corpus
The official strategic documents used for content analysis are public government or institutional documents. To avoid unnecessary duplication of large PDF files and potential licensing ambiguity, the full PDF evidence pack is not placed in the public repository. It is available to editors or reviewers upon request.
